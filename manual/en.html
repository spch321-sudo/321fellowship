Quick Start

Remember these four things and you are ready

The first time, enter your access code to activate the app (page 9). After that, remember these four things — the rest will come naturally.

1. Read the latest news

Open the app — the gold card at the top of Home is the Latest News: camps, topics and prayer requests.

2. Join a meeting

Just below on Home is “Next”. Tap the gold “Join meeting” button — Zoom opens.

3. Read today’s lesson

Tap Resources at the bottom, choose a unit, then a lesson — read it or listen to it.

4. Make the text bigger

Tap A⁺ at the top; each tap is one size bigger: Standard → Large → X-Large → XX-Large.

Install on Android (1)

No Play Store needed — just Chrome

Android phones don’t need the Play Store either. Open the address below in Chrome, then “Add to Home screen” or “Install”.

spch321-sudo.github.io/321fellowship/

1. Open it in Chrome

Tap Chrome (the red, yellow and green circle), type the address at the top — or tap the link a friend sent you.

2. Tap “⋮” at top right

At the far right of the address bar are three dots ⋮ — tap to open the menu.

3. Tap “Add to Home screen”

💡 If you tapped the link inside LINE or WeChat, tap ⋮ or ⋯ at the top right and choose “Open in browser” or “Open in Chrome”, then start from step 2.

Install on Android (2)

Tap “Install” — and you are done

4. Tap “Install”

In the pop-up, tap Install. If you are offered “Create shortcut” or “Install”, choose Install.

5. Find the 321 icon

The gold 321 icon appears on your home screen. If not, swipe up to open your app list, find “321空中團契”, then press and hold to drag it out.

6. Tap to open

It opens full-screen, just like any app. From now on it updates itself — no reinstalling.

【Good to know】Phones sold in mainland China (Huawei, Xiaomi, OPPO, vivo…) may not have Chrome. Use the phone’s own browser, open its menu (☰ or ⋮) and look for “Add to desktop” or “Add to Home screen”.

⚠️ Still seeing an old version? Close the app completely and reopen it; if needed, tap “Check for updates” or “Force update” at the bottom of Settings.

Activate with Your Access Code

Enter it once, the first time you open the app

The first time you open the app you will see the activation screen. Ask the co-worker or small-group leader who invited you, or the fellowship’s serving team, for the access code.

1. The activation screen

It appears the first time you open the app. Choose 繁 / 简 / EN at the top right if you like.

2. Enter the code, tap “Activate”

Tap the white box, type the code you were given, then tap the gold Activate button.

3. Hallelujah! You’re in!

Fireworks celebrate, then Home opens a few seconds later (tap to go straight in). From now on it opens straight away — no need to enter the code again.

💡 If you see “That code isn’t right”, check and try again. Extra spaces or dashes between the digits are fine.

⚠️ A new phone, cleared browser data or a private window will each need the code again. Please don’t post the code publicly.

Questions & Answers

What people ask most

Q　Does the app cost anything?

A　No — it is completely free.

Q　Where do I get the access code?

A　Ask the co-worker or leader who invited you. You enter it once.

Q　The text is too small?

A　Tap A⁺ at the top, or choose XX-Large in Settings → Text Size.

Q　How do I join a meeting?

A　On Home or the Meet tab, tap “Join meeting”. Meeting ID 819 397 2372, passcode 321.

Q　The meeting time looks wrong?

A　The schedule is shown in your local time and converts automatically — trust what the screen shows.

Q　No sound when reading aloud?

A　Check the silent switch and volume; in Settings tap “🔊 Try it” or “Clear Voice Cache”.

Q　The app hasn’t updated?

A　Close it fully and reopen; at the bottom of Settings tap “Check for updates” or “Force update”.

Q　Changing phones?

A　Re-enter the access code; export the 2-3-5 tree, then import it.

Q　Can I use it offline?

A　Pages you have opened mostly work offline; Sage AI, quizzes and meetings need internet.

Q　Sage AI’s answer seems wrong?

A　Sage AI is only a helper — let Scripture be the standard and talk it over with your spiritual leaders.
